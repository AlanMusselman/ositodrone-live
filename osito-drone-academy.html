THE OSITO LEARNING PATHWAY

Students don't just fly-they learn the foundational technology behind modern unmanned aerial systems:

STEP 1

1. ENGINEERING & BUILD

Understand lightweight drone components, 3D-printed ducted frames, brushed/brushless propulsion, and safe 1S battery management.

STEP 2

2. CONFIGURATION & CODE

Learn essential flight controller settings using Betaflight, gyroscope PID stabilization, and low-latency control protocols like ExpressLRS.

STEP 3

3. PILOT TRAINING

Master stick control and spatial awareness in our 4-Tablet Simulator Pod before taking the controls inside our netted pop-up flight tracks.

SAFETY FIRST, ALWAYS

Safety is our top priority for every school, camp, and pop-up event:

FULLY CONTAINED FLIGHT ARENAS All active flying takes place strictly within modular, reinforced floor-to-ceiling safety netting.
SAFE SUB-250G EQUIPMENT We fly lightweight, ducted Tiny Whoop class micro-drones equipped with fully shielded propellers to prevent any impact damage.
PROTECTIVE GEAR & PROTOCOLS All students and participants are provided with protective eyewear and clear pre-flight briefings from our 3 certified instructors.
